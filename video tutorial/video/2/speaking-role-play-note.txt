# DMI Speaking Role Play Practice Now Available

Dear DMI IELTS Students,

DMI Computer Education has added Speaking Role Play practice to the IELTS Band 7+ Preparation Course. These activities help you practise in an exam-style setting with realistic IELTS Speaking questions, especially for Part 1.

## Why This Helps

- Practise answering naturally under time pressure.
- Build confidence, fluency, pronunciation awareness, and timing.
- Reduce fear before the real Speaking test.
- Train with questions that follow IELTS speaking expectations.

## How DMI Uses This Feature

This is not only a video lesson. It is active speaking practice. Students should speak out loud, repeat answers when needed, and use teacher feedback during class or lab practice to improve.

## What To Do

- Open the Speaking section of the DMI LMS.
- Complete each speaking role play activity.
- Answer aloud as if you are in the real IELTS test.
- Ask your DMI teacher for guidance after practice.

Use these role plays regularly to build fluency and exam confidence. DMI Computer Education is here to support your progress toward Band 7 and beyond.
