How to practise
  • Answer each question out loud first, then open the model answer and compare.
  • Learn the ideas and vocabulary. Do not memorise answers word for word, because examiners notice.
  • Part 1: 2–3 sentences. Part 2: speak for the full 2 minutes. Part 3: give a view, a reason and an example.
  • Record yourself on your phone and listen for pauses, grammar and pronunciation.
